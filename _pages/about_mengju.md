**Meng-Ju Hsieh**

Meng-Ju is a PhD student working on foundation models for architecture, exploring how large-scale models can support architectural design and analysis. He is co-supervised by Avishek Anand, [Pei-Yu Wu](https://www.tudelft.nl/staff/p.y.wu/), and [Dr. Michela Turrin](https://www.tudelft.nl/en/staff/m.turrin/).

Research interests:

- Foundation models for architecture
- Generative and computational design
- Machine learning for the built environment
