Course materials

A bachelor's course on the foundations of data mining — how we represent, compare, index, and mine large collections of data. This year the course has an information-access focus, building from distances and similarity through embeddings, matrices, graphs, recommenders, and hashing.

Mondays 13:45 & Thursdays 10:45 · EEMCS Lecture Hall Ampere · labs Tuesdays 08:45 · exam Monday 25 January, 13:30. Teaching team: A. Anand, J. Urbano, N. Tömen, with guest lecturers.
